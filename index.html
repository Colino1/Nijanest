Questions

Is this the finished app?

No. It is an early version built to learn from real tenants and landlords. What you tell us decides what we build first.

Why can't I see the owner's number?

So nobody gets chased or scammed before an inspection. After we confirm your inspection, the contact person's details are shared.

Do I pay anything as a tenant?

Browsing and requesting an inspection are free. When the landlord accepts, you pay a small booking fee to NaijaNest only, never to the landlord or contact person, and we then release the contact. There are no agent fees.

Someone asked me for money. What do I do?

Do not pay. Report it on WhatsApp and we will suspend that listing while we investigate.
